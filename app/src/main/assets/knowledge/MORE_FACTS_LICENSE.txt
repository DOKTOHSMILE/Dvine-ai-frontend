Ibiko's further built-in knowledge (tools/build_more_facts.py writes these files)

country_more.tsv
  Calling codes, internet domains, regions, ISO codes and UN membership of the countries, from
  mledoze/countries (https://github.com/mledoze/countries), made available under the Open Database
  License (ODbL) 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/ . The same source and licence as
  world_facts.tsv (see WORLD_FACTS_LICENSE.txt).

elements_more.tsv
  Atomic mass, category, state at room temperature, discovery, melting and boiling points, density,
  electron configuration, group and period of the 118 elements, from Bowserinator/Periodic-Table-JSON
  (https://github.com/Bowserinator/Periodic-Table-JSON), whose data comes from Wikipedia. Licensed under
  Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).
  This file is an adaptation (selected fields, temperatures also given in degrees Celsius) and is shared
  under the same licence.

local_facts.tsv
  Nigeria's 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory with their capitals, and the eight planets
  (order from the Sun, diameter, length of a year, kind; figures from NASA's planetary fact sheets, which
  are in the public domain). Facts written down by the project.
